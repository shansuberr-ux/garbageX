# GarbageX — Water-Body Waste Removal Device

> **Concept Design · Fusion 360 · NIT Trichy · 🏆 Runner-up / 50 teams**

## Problem Statement

Floating solid waste in lakes, rivers, and coastal waters causes severe environmental and ecological damage. Existing solutions (modified fishing nets, manual collection boats) are labor-intensive, slow, and impractical at scale.

GarbageX proposes a passive-mount, power-assisted solution retrofittable to any existing vessel.

---

## How It Works

1. **Adhesive Belt** — A continuous belt coated with waterproof adhesive faces the water surface at the bow
2. **Collection** — As the vessel moves forward, floating waste contacts the belt and sticks to it
3. **Scraper** — A blade removes collected waste from the belt without stripping the adhesive
4. **Waste Bin** — Scraped waste falls into a collection container below
5. **Adhesive Replenishment** — A sprayer nozzle recoats the belt continuously
6. **Belt Drive** — Electric motor powers the belt via a roller system
